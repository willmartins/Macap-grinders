# White redesign

Will's note: the live site was too dark, and too much of it was a full-width photograph. This pass makes white the default and lets type and the framed object carry the page. Yellow stays a 3px seam and a focus mark. The references are unchanged: Polestar, Teenage Engineering, On Running, New Balance, on white space.

The professional theme uses the same white ground as home and neutral. A black page is no longer the look of a bar grinder, a collection, or the professional journey.

## What changed

- Home is a white introduction and two choices. Each choice has a framed photograph, not a viewport-tall panel. The yellow seam is the gap between the two cards.
- Journey, collection, knowledge and product openings are a type block beside a framed image. The name, the figures, Enquire and Compare stay in that first block on a model page.
- Chapter photographs (image and text) sit in the content frame at a 4:3 ratio, on an alloy plinth. They no longer run to the edge of the screen.
- The product gallery is the same: framed, in the content width, with a peek on a phone. The black band is gone.
- The header stays solid. It no longer turns white type over a photograph.

## Templates

Home, Home grinders, Professional, collections, the group catalogue (it follows the white chrome), grinder pages, knowledge hub, posts, about and contact. Legal and compare were already type on white. The footer follows the page ground, so it is white on every template.

Full-width imagery is limited to nothing on these templates. Photographs stay, inside a frame.
